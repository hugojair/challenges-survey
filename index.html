DMLR Survey Chapter · 2023

Academic
Competitions

Hugo Jair Escalante · INAOE & UT El Paso · Aleksandra Kruchinina · Université Paris Saclay
Competitions comprise effective means for advancing the state of the art, putting in the spotlight of a scientific community specific topics and problems, and closing the gap for under-represented communities. This chapter surveys academic challenges in the context of machine learning and related fields — reviewing the most influential competitions, analyzing challenges per area of knowledge, and outlining open questions for the years ahead.
